About me

Three subjects that AI training depends on, each studied to master's level.

Mathematics

Step-by-step reasoning

  • Follow a multi-step solution line by line and find the first wrong step
  • Check numerical answers for accuracy and stability
  • Algebra, analysis, topology, differential equations and numerical methods at master's level

Physics

Physical-world understanding

  • Decide whether a robot's action is physically possible
  • Check cause and effect with mechanics, electromagnetism and thermodynamics
  • Use units, conservation laws and sanity checks on results

Computer Science

Code, plans and agents

  • Trace programs, algorithms and data structures to verify what they do
  • Know how search, planning and intelligent agents work
  • Algorithms, artificial intelligence and computational intelligence at master's level

Agentic AI training

AI that plans and acts in several steps

Agentic systems plan and carry out a job in several steps. I can review the plan, the reasoning behind each step and the final result.

Robotics AI training

AI that works with the physical world

Robotic tasks involve physical systems, where physics decides what is correct. I can check actions and sequences against mechanics and cause and effect.

Research

My research direction sits where the three subjects meet. It is also how I look at agentic and robotic AI: equations for the reasoning, physics for the real world, algorithms for the plan.

Proposed research direction

Computational and Mathematical Modelling of Complex Physical Systems

The aim is to describe complex physical systems with mathematical models, check those models against the principles of physics, and solve them with algorithms and simulations.

  1. Mathematics

    Model

    Describe physical systems with mathematical equations.

    Focus areas

    • Nonlinear dynamics

  2. Physics

    Focus areas
